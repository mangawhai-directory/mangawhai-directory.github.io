## Creative specs

| Placement | Desktop | Mobile | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage tile | 1152 × 400 px | 500 × 500 px | JPG / PNG / SVG |
| Category header | 500 × 250 px | 500 × 500 px | JPG / PNG / SVG |
| Premium listing image | 800 × 800 px square | — | JPG / PNG |

- **Under 200 KB** compressed. SVG is fine for logos and simple graphics.
- **Mobile creative is optional but strongly recommended** — most of our traffic is mobile, and a purpose-built crop reads far better than a scaled desktop banner.
- **Responsive scaling.** Listed dimensions are the intrinsic creative size; your ad scales proportionally to the visitor's viewport. Design for the listed size and keep important text 12 px inside every edge.
- **One destination URL per creative** — your website, a phone number, or a booking link.
- **Premium blurb:** 25–30 words. Shorter usually reads better.
- We approve creative before it goes live, and come back to you within a couple of business days if anything needs changing.

{{< note title="No artwork ready?" >}}
We design ads for advertisers who'd rather not do it themselves — a one-off cost, quoted before we start. We can also crop a premium listing image from your existing branding at no extra charge.
{{< /note >}}

## Booking, payment and cancellation

**How to book.** [Contact us](/contact/) with the placement you want, the category if you're taking a category header, and the months you'd like to run. We'll confirm availability, send an invoice, and put you live on the first day of your booked month. Get in early for peak periods — summer and school holidays go first.

**Term.** Bookings are for one or more whole calendar months that you nominate — just March, or March through May. Each booked month runs from the first to the last day of that month. No rolling subscriptions and no auto-renewal: when your booked period ends the ad comes down unless you book again. You can book any months starting within the next 12 months, and we cap it there so nobody holds today's price against a spot years away.

**Payment.** We invoice for the full booked period on confirmation. Your ad goes live on the first day of the first booked month, provided we've cleared payment and approved creative by then.

**Included with every booking.** One free creative swap per month, a performance report at the end of each booked month, and a plain invoice.

## Worth knowing up front

- **Advertising doesn't buy ranking.** Paid placement never changes the order of listings on a category page.
- **Exclusivity is real but time-bound.** One advertiser per category and one homepage tile per month, for the months you've booked. When your period ends the slot is open to anyone.
- **Premium content stays live** at no further cost until you change it — see our [terms](/terms/) for what happens if we ever change the offering.
- **Reporting is aggregate.** You get impression and click totals for your placement. We never pass on information about individual visitors, and we don't run behavioural tracking or retargeting.
- **We won't run** animated or auto-playing creative, anything mimicking site navigation or a system alert, third-party tracking pixels, or anything misleading under the Fair Trading Act.
- **Your statutory rights stand.** Nothing here limits rights that can't lawfully be excluded under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.
