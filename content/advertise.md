## Why advertise here

Mangawhai Directory is the local-first business directory for Mangawhai, Mangawhai Heads, Te Arai, Kaiwaka and surrounds. Visitors land here because they're already looking for someone to call — a builder, a cafe, a plumber, a place to stay. There's no scroll of sponsored noise above the listings, so a single ad on the page actually gets seen.

Every business gets a **free organic listing**. Paid ads simply put your brand in the highest-traffic spots: above the category page that matches your service, or on the homepage where everyone lands first.

## Packages

### Premium listing — $30+gst / month

Make your free listing stand out without changing where it sits in the category list.

- A square **image or logo** displayed to the left of your name and blurb.
- Same listing position as the free version — no reordering, no badges.
- Bring your own image or we can crop one from your existing branding.
- **Image spec:** square (e.g. 400 × 400 px), JPG or PNG under 200 KB.
- **Blurb:** keep it under 20-25 words.

### Category page header — $150+gst / month

A banner placed at the top of one category page (e.g. *Builders & Renovations*, *Cafes & Restaurants*, *Plumbers*). **Note**: This can be any category you like, it doesn't have to be the one you are in.

- **Desktop creative:** 500 × 250 px
- **Mobile creative:** 500 × 500 px (recommended) — scales to fit the viewport
- Highest-intent placement — people on a category page are actively choosing who to call.
- Static image plus a click-through to your website, phone or booking link.
- One advertiser per category at a time.

### Homepage tile — $400+gst / month

A wide tile placed below the hero on the homepage.

- **Desktop creative:** 1152 × 400 px
- **Mobile creative:** 500 × 500 px (recommended) — scales to fit the viewport
- Brand-builder placement — every visitor to the directory passes through here.
- Static image plus a click-through.
- Limited slots; rotates evenly with other homepage advertisers.

## Creative specs

- **Format:** JPG or PNG (under 200 KB compressed). SVG accepted for logos and simple graphics.
- **Mobile creative is optional but recommended.** If you only supply the desktop version we'll scale it to fit, but a purpose-built mobile crop reads much better on a phone — most of our traffic is mobile.
- **Responsive scaling.** Listed dimensions are the intrinsic creative size — your ad scales proportionally to the visitor's viewport. On most desktops the homepage tile renders at roughly its full 1152 × 400, but it may scale down a little on narrower screens. Design for the listed size and the scaling will look clean.
- Keep the most important text away from the edges (a 12 px safe area inside each ad).
