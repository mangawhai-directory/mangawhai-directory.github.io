The medical and dental practices here cover GPs, the local medical centre, dentists, physiotherapists and allied health providers based in Mangawhai and Mangawhai Heads. After-hours and emergency cover is limited compared with Whangārei or Warkworth — most practices route urgent calls through Healthline or the closest emergency department, so it's worth knowing the closest after-hours options if you're staying in the area. Enrolling with a local GP if you're moving to the region usually takes a few weeks; most practices below note enrolment availability on their websites or by direct contact.
