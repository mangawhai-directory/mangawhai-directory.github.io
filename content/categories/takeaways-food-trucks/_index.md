Quick food in Mangawhai means fish and chips at the Heads, the rotating food trucks that set up near the surf beach in summer, and a handful of takeaway kitchens that work alongside the village pubs and cafés. This list covers operators across Mangawhai, Mangawhai Heads, Kaiwaka and Te Arai who sell food to take away or run mobile food trailers at markets and events. Hours change with the season — most truck operators post daily on Facebook, so the contact links here are usually the fastest way to find out what's open tonight.
