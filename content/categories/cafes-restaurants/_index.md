Mangawhai's eating scene runs from harbourside cafés to weekend-busy restaurants, and most places lean on local growers, the Pakiri coast and Kaipara dairy producers for what they put on the plate. The cafés below cover Mangawhai Village, Mangawhai Heads and the surrounding rural blocks, with a mix of all-day brunch spots, family-friendly lunch stops and dinner options that take bookings on summer Saturdays. If you're after coffee on the way through, look first at the village; for a longer sit-down meal, the Heads has more of the evening trade.
