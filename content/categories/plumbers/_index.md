Plumbers and gasfitters listed here cover Mangawhai, Mangawhai Heads, Kaiwaka and the rural area between Te Arai and the Brynderwyns. Local jobs run the usual range — hot water cylinder swaps, leaks, septic and stormwater work for properties not on town supply, bathroom and laundry renovations, and gas installs for new builds. After-hours callouts are limited around here; if it's an emergency outside business hours, ring more than one of the numbers below. Most operators are also able to advise on water-tank arrangements, which most properties outside the village rely on.
