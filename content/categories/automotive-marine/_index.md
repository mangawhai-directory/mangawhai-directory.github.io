Workshops and trades in this category cover both vehicles and boats — mechanical servicing, WoF, panel and paint, tyre and battery, plus the marine side that matters in a coastal town: outboard servicing, antifouling, trailer repair and boat-rigging work. Mangawhai's harbour and the regular launch traffic at the Heads keep the marine operators busy through summer. If you're towing in for a job, most workshops below take bookings rather than walk-ins; the marine operators in particular tend to schedule around tides and the haul-out window, so book early in the season.
