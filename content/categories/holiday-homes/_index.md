The bach economy is a big part of Mangawhai's summer rhythm, and the operators below are the people you contact for a stay in a privately-owned holiday home. Some manage portfolios across Mangawhai, Mangawhai Heads, Te Arai and Pakiri; others let a single house directly. Bookings tend to lock in months ahead for the December–January peak, with weekends through autumn easier to secure. If you're after a hotel or B&B instead, see the Accommodation category. Most listings here also handle keys, linen and the occasional summer-storm clean-up.
