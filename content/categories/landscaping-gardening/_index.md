Landscaping and gardening businesses in this category cover both regular maintenance — lawn care, hedge trimming, section tidies for holiday-home owners — and design-build work like new gardens, retaining walls and irrigation. Mangawhai sections range from sandy coastal sites at Mangawhai Heads to clay-heavy rural blocks toward Kaiwaka, and the operators below are used to working with both. If you're an absentee owner who needs the place looking sharp by the time you next drive up from Auckland, most of the listed gardeners will work to a recurring schedule and send a photo afterwards.
