Property managers in Mangawhai handle two quite separate kinds of work: long-term residential tenancies for landlords who own here but live elsewhere, and short-term holiday-let management for owners of bach properties at Mangawhai Heads, Te Arai and along the harbour. The companies below split between those two specialties; some do both. If you're an absentee owner, most managers offer monthly statements, maintenance coordination with local tradies, and a key-handling service for guests. Tenancy compliance with the Healthy Homes standards is now standard — ask each operator what's included.
