This category gathers the activity operators around Mangawhai and Mangawhai Heads — fishing charters, guided walks across the heads to the cliff lookout, mini-golf, kayak hires, scenic flights out of Kaipara airfields and the seasonal events that local businesses run. For surf-specific operators see Surf, Beach & Watersports; for places to eat between activities see Cafés & Restaurants. Most of the bookings here are best done direct rather than through aggregator sites, since small operators frequently shift schedules around weather and tides on the Mangawhai bar.
