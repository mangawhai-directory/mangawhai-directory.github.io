This category covers the established stays in Mangawhai and Mangawhai Heads — motels, B&Bs, lodges, boutique hotels and the bigger accommodation businesses that take bookings year-round. For privately-let beach houses and Airbnb-style rentals, see Holiday Homes & Short-Term Rentals; the split is deliberate because the booking experience and contact points are quite different. Most of the operators below sit close to the harbour or within a short drive of the surf beach, with peak-season rates running from late December through January and a noticeable shoulder-season window worth asking about.
