Mangawhai's drinking spots cover the Tavern, a couple of bistro bars in the village, summer-only pop-ups near Mangawhai Heads and a small number of independent breweries operating out of nearby Kaiwaka and the Kaipara coast. The list below is the place to start if you're sorting a Friday meal, planning a stop on a Northland brewery loop or working out where takes bookings for groups. Expect shorter hours through winter, with most venues running full kitchens from Labour Weekend through to Easter and tapering off afterwards.
