Community groups, sports clubs, churches, marae and volunteer organisations in Mangawhai and Mangawhai Heads are listed here. The local volunteering and club scene runs deep — surf life saving at the Heads, the hall committees, the rugby and netball clubs, environmental and harbour care groups, the Mangawhai Activity Zone, and the various places of worship across denominations. Most are run by volunteers who answer email and Facebook messages on their own time. If you're new to the area, this is often the fastest way to meet people and find the rhythm of the place beyond the summer rush.
