Mangawhai Heads is the centre of the local surf scene, and this category lists the surf schools, board and wetsuit hire shops, kayak and SUP rental operators, and the lesson providers who teach in the bay or out on the open beach at Te Arai. The Mangawhai bar is tidal and conditions change quickly — most operators below take bookings the same week rather than far in advance, and they're the ones to ring for genuine local read on conditions. Coverage extends from Mangawhai Heads down to Pakiri for the schools that run mobile lessons.
