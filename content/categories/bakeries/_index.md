The bakeries listed here cover bread, pastries, pies and the long-standing Mangawhai morning queue. You'll find both established storefronts in Mangawhai Village and Mangawhai Heads and smaller cottage producers who sell through the markets and a few stockists. If you're catering for a group — beach gatherings, weddings at Tara Iti, family weekends in Kaiwaka — most operators below take orders ahead by a day or two. Sourdough loaves often sell out before lunch in summer, so it's worth a phone call rather than driving across to the Heads on spec.
