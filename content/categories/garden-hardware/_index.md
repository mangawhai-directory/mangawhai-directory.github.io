Garden centres, plant nurseries, hardware stores and trade suppliers serving Mangawhai and Mangawhai Heads are listed in this category. Coverage runs from the small village garden retailers and bag-mix yards through to larger trade-orientated suppliers up at Kaiwaka and across to Wellsford. Locally-grown stock is common, especially for natives suited to the coastal sections around the Heads. For bigger landscaping projects most operators will deliver to site; for DIY weekends, the in-store ranges cover the usual fixings, paint, garden tools and weekend project materials.
