The painters and decorators in this list work across Mangawhai, Mangawhai Heads and the surrounding coastal blocks. Coastal exposure does the usual things to weatherboard and roof iron — most of the trade here is exterior repaints on a 7–10 year cycle, plus interior decorating for new-build handovers and renovations. Wallpapering and specialty finishes are handled by a smaller subset of the operators below. Booking ahead is sensible; the better-known crews carry waitlists of 6–12 weeks through the warmer months and slow down in winter when conditions stop exterior work.
